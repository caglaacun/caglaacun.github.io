I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering at Hacettepe University. I completed my Ph.D. at the Knowledge Discovery and Web Mining Lab with [Dr. Olfa Nasraoui](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SGscZDgAAAAJ&hl=en) at the University of Louisville.

My research is in explainable machine learning, with the aim of making black-box models more transparent and trustworthy. Rather than attaching explanations to a model only after it has been trained, I develop **in-training** and **pre-hoc/co-hoc** explainability frameworks, in which an interpretable component is learned jointly with the predictive model so that the resulting explanations are faithful to the model by construction. Current directions include concept-based explanation for deep architectures, the evaluation of explanation faithfulness beyond deletion-based metrics, and applications to robotic systems.
